05 · Method and limits

How it was done

  • Data. All 2024 trips from Citi Bike. Removed ? of ? rows: out-of-month spillover, missing stations, non-positive durations, trips over 24 hours. Weather: NOAA, Central Park. Bike lanes and borough boundaries: NYC Open Data.
  • Weather. Regression of log daily rides on weekday, holiday, temperature, rain timing, snow and a growth trend. Intervals from a 14-day block bootstrap. These are associations in one year of data, not controlled experiments.
  • Rebalancing. Average net flow on ? non-holiday weekdays. The morning and evening flows correlate at ? after dividing by each station's traffic.
  • Forecast. Poisson gradient boosting, ? trees, settings chosen on September–October. Every input is known a day ahead. Weather inputs are observed, not forecast, so weather's contribution is an upper bound.
  • Limits. One year. No dock capacity or live station status. Station names serve as IDs.
  • Code. 2024 notebook, build scripts, and a member-vs-casual classifier. Every number in this text is checked against the data in CI.

Data: Citi Bike System Data, NOAA NCEI, NYC Open Data. Basemaps: Esri, HERE, Garmin, © OpenStreetMap contributors.
